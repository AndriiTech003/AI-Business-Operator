# Rubric: payment reminder e-mail

Graded 1–5. Each criterion below is worth the same; the score is 1 + 4 × (criteria met / criteria).

- [personalized] Greets the contact by first name, or the company team when no contact is known.
- [context] Names the invoice number.
- [amount] States the open amount with currency.
- [due_date] States the due date in ISO format.
- [polite] Polite and non-threatening (no "final warning", "legal action", "immediately").
- [cta] Asks for payment or a reply if something is unclear.
- [signature] Signs off with the sender's name and company.
- [no_placeholders] No template placeholders.
